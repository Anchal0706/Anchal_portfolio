The Photoreal Studio (Gaussian Splat)

Gaussian Splatting KHR_gaussian_splatting glTF photoreal volumetric

Capture Anchal's actual workspace with a 3-minute phone video. Convert to a 3D Gaussian splat — millions of fuzzy ellipsoids that render photoreal, in real-time, in the browser. Visitors fly through a real-feeling space; her actual notebook, certifications, and bookshelf become hover-targets that open content.

Why now: Khronos finalized the glTF Gaussian-splat extension in Feb 2026. Almost no portfolios use this yet. It looks like XR, runs on a phone, and is unmistakably "next."

05 · MOST CONTRARIAN (the "anti-AI" play)

The Reading Room (Tactile Rebellion)

Creative Bloq 2026 trend paper · ink · weight 3D as restraint, not spectacle

The contrarian play. While everyone races toward more pixels, Anchal's site goes the other direction — tactile rebellion, the dominant 2026 design trend per Creative Bloq. Real paper textures, ink that bleeds, type carved into wood, leather grain. The "3D" is sub-perceptual — pages have weight, ink has thickness, light hits like a desk lamp.

The provocative bet: a content strategist who explicitly says "I value clarity over spectacle" stands out more by not doing AI-aesthetic. Hermès digital × New Yorker × your favorite indie press. Refined, expensive-looking, almost zero competition.
